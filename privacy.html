Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

Pulse Internet Speed Test does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any personal information.

What data Pulse stores

Pulse saves your last 20 speed-test results (download speed, upload speed, ping, jitter and the time of the test) using Chrome's local storage. These results stay in your own browser and never leave your device. You can delete them at any time with the "Clear" button in the extension.

How the speed test works

To measure your speed, Pulse downloads and uploads random test data to Cloudflare's public speed-test service at speed.cloudflare.com. As with any website you visit, Cloudflare receives your IP address to deliver that data. Pulse does not send Cloudflare anything else about you. Cloudflare's own privacy policy applies to those connections.

Permissions

  • storage: saves your test history on your device.
  • speed.cloudflare.com: sends and receives test data. Pulse cannot read or change any other website.

What Pulse does not do

  • No analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No access to your browsing history or the pages you visit.
